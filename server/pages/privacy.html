BlueMob privacy policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

In short: BlueMob works phone to phone. Your messages and calls are end-to-end encrypted: nobody else, including BlueMob, can read or hear them. Most of what you put in BlueMob stays encrypted on your phone. The BlueMob server only holds what's needed to deliver messages to people who aren't nearby, and you can delete it any time.

Who we are

BlueMob ("we") is responsible for this app and its server. Grievance officer under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and contact for all privacy questions: {{OFFICER}}{{EMAIL}}. We reply within 7 days and resolve requests within 30 days.

What stays on your phone

Your name, profile photo emoji, mobile number, date of birth, sex, blood group, allergies, medical conditions, SOS contacts, chats, files, call history, trips and positions, guides read and app usage. All of it is encrypted on the phone with a key kept in the phone's secure hardware. We can't see it.

We don't sell data, show ads or track you across apps.

How long we keep it

Messages and files waiting on the server: until delivered, 7 days at most. Backups: the newest 3. Reports: the newest 500. Your key, number fingerprint and email: until you delete your account. On phones, when a trip ends its tracking stops, and the record of who reached which checkpoint is erased 30 days later. The trip group and its chat stay until each person leaves, so people can keep in touch. Field-test results are erased 30 days after the test. Each time BlueMob staff open an admin page or look up a screenshot or trip code, it's logged, and the log is kept for a year.

Your rights

You can see and change your details in the app (You → Account), turn off location sharing, background running and automatic error reports, and delete your account at any time: You → Account → Delete my account. That removes your ID, number fingerprint, email, backups and waiting messages from the server, and everything from your phone. See how to delete your account. Under India's DPDP Act and the EU GDPR you may also ask us to access, correct or erase your data, or complain to us at {{EMAIL}}. In India you may then approach the Data Protection Board of India.

Children

BlueMob is for people aged 13 and over. Under-18s in India should use it with a parent's or guardian's consent.

Safety

BlueMob adds help from people nearby but can't guarantee that an SOS is received. In an emergency, also call 112 (India) or your local emergency number.

Changes

If this policy changes, the app will tell you in "News from BlueMob" before the change applies.
