Delete your BlueMob account

In the app (fastest):
  1. Open BlueMob → You → Account & backup.
  2. Tap Delete my account, type DELETE and confirm.
This removes your BlueMob ID, public key, number fingerprint, email, cloud backups and any messages waiting for you from the BlueMob server, and wipes everything from your phone.
Without the app (lost phone, uninstalled): email {{EMAIL}} from the email address on your account, or with your BlueMob ID, and ask us to delete it. We do it within 30 days and confirm by email.

Not deleted: messages you already sent stay on the phones of the people you sent them to, and ratings you gave stay with the people you rated, without your name if your account is deleted.
