# BlueMob for trek operators, yatra organisers and volunteer groups

**One line:** your group's phones keep reaching each other — messages, roll calls and SOS — where there is no
network. Free for SOS, no ads, works in Hindi and English.

## The problem you already have

- On high trails and in valleys there is no signal for hours. A lost or injured member can't call the leader.
- At melas and big gatherings the network jams. Families get separated and phones show full bars but nothing goes.
- Roll calls at camp or at a meeting point are done by shouting names.

## What BlueMob does for a group

1. **Trip group:** before setting off, everyone scans one QR code. That's the whole setup.
2. **Roll call:** the leader taps once; every phone answers by itself, and the leader sees who was reached — directly,
   through other members' phones, or over the internet if anyone has it.
3. **SOS:** five presses of the power button, a missed check-in or a hard fall sends an SOS to everyone in range, and
   every phone that hears it passes it on. The emergency card on the lock screen shows blood group and who to call.
4. **Offline guide:** first aid, altitude sickness, hypothermia, getting lost — on the phone, in Hindi too.

## What we ask of a partner

- Add "install BlueMob and join the trip group" to your pre-trip checklist and briefing (5 minutes).
- Let us run one **field day** with one of your batches (docs/field-day.md): each phone measures delivery, speed,
  battery and whether the phone stopped the app. You get the results.
- Tell us what failed. We fix it before the next season.

## What we don't claim

BlueMob does not replace 112, a satellite messenger or a trained guide. Phone-to-phone range is tens of metres,
more when members are spread along a trail. It works best when the whole group installs it **before** the trip.
